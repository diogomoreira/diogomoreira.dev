## Professor

I started my career as a professor at the **Federal Institute of Education, Science and Technology of Paraíba** in 2014 — the same campus where I did my undergraduate degree. Over these 12 years I've lectured a number of subjects, among them:

- **Markup Languages** (HTML and CSS)
- **Web Scripting Languages** (JavaScript/Web programming fundamentals)
- **Web Programming** (React, Service Workers, Microfrontends and SPAs)
- **Software Architecture and Design Patterns** (GoF patterns and architectural styles)
- **Configuration and Change Management** (Version control systems and change management practices)
- **Software Testing** (Planning, writing and running white-box/black-box tests with automated tooling)
- **Systems Analysis and Design** (Lifecycle models, software development processes and UML)

It's good to be in the classroom, and I feel it really pushes me to be a better person and professional every day.
