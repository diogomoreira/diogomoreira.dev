Welcome to my **digital garden**!

A "digital garden" is an online space that combines elements of a notebook and a blog. It is a place to share thoughts and ideas intended to be cultivated/evolved in public.

Unlike traditional blogs, where posts have publication dates and can become outdated, a digital garden prioritizes content that is—in most cases—continuously edited and refined over time.

This space is constantly evolving, just like my interests and ideas.
