## Social Media

I’ve been _trying_ to adopt healthier habits regarding social media consumption this year and, consequently, stepping away from the world of Big Tech as much as possible.

One thing I’ve been doing—and which I believe is working to cut down my social media usage—is spending at least 1 to 2 hours without using my phone after waking up. I check notifications, see if there’s anything urgent (a payment, a message from relatives, or something similar), and then put it aside. This has even helped me get back into reading and "slow down" a bit.

Last year, I completely deactivated my old Twitter account and started using [Mastodon](https://hachyderm.io/@diogomoreira) as a "microblog" substitute. I deleted my profile and recreated it solely to "reserve" my username and prevent anyone from impersonating me. I also deleted my Facebook profile, which I wasn't using anymore.
