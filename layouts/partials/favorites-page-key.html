{{/* Usage: `partial "favorites-page-key.html" .` — the data/favorites/ key a /favorites page renders, or "" when it has none. An adapter page carries it as `collection`; a hand-written one falls back to its own filename, so `books.md` needs no such param. An adapter page has no `.File` at all, hence the `with` rather than a `default` — Go templates evaluate both arms of that.
