{{/* Usage: `if partial "is-external-link.html" $url`target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`end` The one place a link's off-site-ness is decided. Returns "true" when off-site and "" otherwise; callers own their own attributes, so `social.html` keeps its `rel="me"`. - **It gates on the scheme**, which excludes `mailto:` and `tel:` for free — they parse to an empty Host and never reach the comparison. - **The host comes from `baseURL`**, where the ↗ marker rule in main.css has to hardcode the domain. The two therefore disagree for an *absolute self-link* under `hugo server`.
